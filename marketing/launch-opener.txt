Introducing sealed.cash

Every onchain payment is a public receipt.
Salary. Rent. Last night's dinner. Forever, on an explorer.

Shielding one transfer doesn't fix it. The next spend from a normal wallet stitches the trail back together.

Sealed is one private balance on @Starknet plus a card that spends from it. The merchant sees a card number. The explorer sees nothing useful.

Not a mixer. A private checking account you can spend from.
