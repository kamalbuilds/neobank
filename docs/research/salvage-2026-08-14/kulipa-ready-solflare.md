## Sourced from pages it fetched

### Solflare official

https://www.solflare.com/blog/cards-service-pause/
Published stamp on the fetch: 2026-06-29 (page also states the stop date as 28 July 2026).

Solflare: partner Kulipa "struggled to keep its operations going" and can no longer support Solflare Card. Virtual and physical cards stopped 28 July 2026. Subscription auto-renewals stop. New partner already being integrated, expected August. Users will create a new card. Partner name not stated. Product page https://www.solflare.com/crypto-card/ still shows paused.

### The Defiant

https://thedefiant.io/converge/cefi/ready-shuts-card-program-after-issuer-winds-down
Published 2026-07-30.

Ready (formerly Argent) shut the card after the issuer wound down with no notice. Itamar Lesuisse: "We were given no notice." Eligible subscriptions refunded. User assets unaffected because wallets are self-custodial.

Issuer: Kulipa, Paris-based, card programs for roughly 20 crypto wallets and fintechs. Solflare co-founder Vidor Bojthe: Kulipa winding down due to solvency issues.

Same article: on June 16 Ready cut card service for users outside the EEA with about an hour's notice, after Kulipa shifted to a new issuing partner licensed only for European jurisdictions. That is the June EEA-only restriction.

Both Ready and Solflare cards pulled from the user wallet at purchase, so no customer balances sat at Kulipa when it failed.
